## Most In-Demand Skills Summary

### Technical skills — frequency across 5 postings

| Skill | Appears in | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **SQL** | 5/5 postings | 🔴 Critical |
| **Python** | 4/5 postings | 🔴 Critical |
| **Power BI** | 2/5 postings | 🟡 Important |
| **ETL** | 2/5 postings | 🟡 Important |
| **Advanced Excel** | 2/5 postings | 🟡 Important |
| **Cloud (GCP/AWS)** | 1/5 postings | 🟢 Desirable |
| **Tableau** | 1/5 postings | 🟢 Desirable |
| **Prompt Engineering** | 1/5 postings | 🟢 Emerging |

### Work modality
- 5/5 postings are **hybrid** — none are 100% on-site
- High probability of partial remote work from Concepción

### English
- Only 1/5 postings explicitly mentions it (intermediate level)
- Not a hard requirement for entry-level in the Chilean market
- Becomes important in multinational companies and senior roles

### Top hiring sectors
1. **Fintech and digital payments** — highest growth
2. **Traditional banking** — more stability and benefits
3. **IT consulting** — project variety, good early experience

---

## Conclusions for the Study Plan

1. **SQL is skill #1** — mandatory in most postings. Top priority.
2. **Python is #2** — almost as important as SQL already.
3. **Power BI already in my toolkit** — real competitive advantage over other entry-level candidates.
4. **Hybrid modality dominates** — working from Concepción for a Santiago company is perfectly viable.
5. **Cloud and Prompt Engineering emerging** — add basic AWS or GCP as a future plus.
6. **English not a barrier in Chile** — but adds points at multinationals.
