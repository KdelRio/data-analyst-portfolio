\## Salary Ranges — Data Analyst Chile 2026

| Level | Experience | Monthly Salary (CLP) | Notes |

|-------|------------|----------------------|-------|

| \*\*Junior\*\* | 0–2 years | $950.000 – $1.200.000 | Entry level, 1–2 certifications |

| \*\*Mid / Semi Senior\*\* | 2–4 years | $1.400.000 – $2.000.000 | Solid SQL + Python + BI |

| \*\*Senior\*\* | 4+ years | $2.100.000 – $3.100.000 | Leadership + advanced stack |

| \*\*Power Platform Consultant\*\* | 2+ years | $2.000.000 – $3.500.000 | PL-300 + PL-200 required |



\*\*Key notes:\*\*

\- Santiago pays 15–20% more than other regions

\- PL-300 certification significantly increases salary even at junior level

\- Fintech and tech companies pay the most

\- Traditional banking offers more stability and benefits

| Skill | Appears in | Priority |

| Tableau | 1/5 postings | 🟢 Desirable |

| Prompt Engineering | 1/5 postings | 🟢 Emerging |



\### Work modality

\- 5/5 postings are hybrid — none are 100% on-site

\- High probability of partial remote work from Concepción



\### English

\- Only 1/5 postings explicitly mentions it (intermediate level)

\- Not a hard requirement for entry-level in the Chilean market

\- Becomes important in multinational companies and senior roles



\### Top hiring sectors

\- Fintech and digital payments — highest growth

\- Traditional banking — more stability and benefits

\- IT consulting — project variety, good early experience

\## Conclusions for the Study Plan

\- \*\*SQL is skill #1\*\* — mandatory in most postings. Top priority.

\- \*\*Python is #2\*\* — almost as important as SQL already.

\- \*\*Power BI already in my toolkit\*\* — real competitive advantage over other entry-level candidates.

\- \*\*Hybrid modality dominates\*\* — working from Concepción for a Santiago company is perfectly viable.

\- \*\*Cloud and Prompt Engineering emerging\*\* — add basic AWS or GCP as a future plus.

\- \*\*English not a barrier in Chile\*\* — but adds points at multinationals.

\- \*\*PL-300 is the key differentiator\*\* — most requested certification in LATAM according to 2026 analysis.
